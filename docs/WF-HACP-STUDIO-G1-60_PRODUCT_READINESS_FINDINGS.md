# G1-60 Product Readiness Findings

## Product Value Delivered
- **Capability**: `StorefrontFormSubmissionBridgeEngine.ts` (**Form Submission & Lead Capture Bridge Engine**).
- **Product Impact**: Directly enables non-technical users to capture and validate visitor inquiries, contact form submissions, and newsletter signups on published sites and storefronts, mapping `{ name, email, message, subject }` directly to backend endpoint `/api/contact`.
