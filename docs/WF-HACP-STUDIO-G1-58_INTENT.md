# G1-58 Intent & Mission Contract

## Intent
Connect product card CTA actions ('Add to Cart') to persistent cart state, quantity updates, cart drawer toggles, multi-page router transitions (`/store` -> `/cart` -> `/checkout`), shipping address validation, and payment gateway handoff boundaries (`OrderIntentDTO`) via `StorefrontCartCheckoutDrawerEngine.ts`.

## Product Selection
- **Selected Capability**: `StorefrontCartCheckoutDrawerEngine.ts`
- **Product North Star**: "A non-programmer customer can visually create an ecommerce store where products participate in a coherent cart and checkout journey."
